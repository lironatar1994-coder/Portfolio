# LA webs — design (v3, September 2026)

This records the redesign implemented in `src/styles.css`, `src/index.html`, `src/app.js`, `src/projects.mjs` and `scripts/build.mjs`. Product truth is in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md); the reference research is in [docs/studio-research.md](docs/studio-research.md). CSS is the source of truth for values.

## Direction

A bright, high-contrast canvas that lets the work carry the color. The studio's own surfaces are near-white paper and near-black ink with one vermilion accent. Every project section takes the client's real brand color (sampled from the live site, see `docs/project-longcaptures.json`), so darkness and color appear only where the work brings them. This follows what Israeli studios serving brands and small businesses do (light canvases), rather than the dark canvases common among startup-facing studios.

Identity is typographic: the live-text wordmark `LA webs` is set in Frank Ruhl Libre with a vermilion square "period" that recurs as kicker bullet, list marker and cursor dot. An outlined LA monogram from the same font at weight 800 is used for the favicon, the mobile fan's back card and the homepage sharing card (`scripts/share-card.html`, rendered to `public/images/la-webs-share-v3.jpg`: three project cards on a vermilion block beside the headline on paper). The header/footer wordmarks remain live text and case pages retain their own sharing images. See `docs/monogram.md` for asset sources and regeneration. The previous asterisk mark was retired.

## Less copy, stronger hierarchy

The hero and work-grid hierarchy remain unchanged. The website-value section has one heading and three concise benefits. Real WhatsApp previews provide the visual evidence; there are no separate process or hypothetical-question lists.

## Header

The header pills sit over every project color, so they slide away while the visitor scrolls down and return on the first scroll up (an `is-hidden` class toggled from a passive scroll listener, never while the menu is open or a header control has focus). This keeps the dark and vermilion sections clean and stops the pills covering section titles.

## Phones

Phones get their own composition rather than a squeezed desktop. **Screen rhythm (15 September 2026):** on phones the text hero and the hand of cards are two separate full screens (`min-height:min(100svh,900px)`, content centred; the cap keeps tall desktop windows from inflating), and the contact block is a full screen at every width. The phone hero headline scales with both width and height (`min(14.5vw,9svh)`) so it fills its screen. The only button on the first screen is the WhatsApp pill (the work link is a text link, hidden on phones), and the top of a five-card fan (`.hero-peek`, the same card faces as the hand, a link to the work grid) rises from the bottom edge of the first screen, so the first thing a visitor sees is real work and the invitation to scroll is the cards themselves rather than a text cue. **Peek to fan (scroll-triggered, not scroll-linked):** the hand's cards are hidden (`is-waiting`) until the hand is 30% in view; then the script measures the peek and the fan, sets `--from-y` and `--from-s`, fades the peek and replays the `deal` keyframe from the peek's place and size with a small overshoot, staggered by fan position. Leaving the screen resets it, so the deal replays on every visit. Nothing is tied to scroll position, which is what felt jerky on iPhones before. **Landing on screens:** phones (760px wide or less **and** 900px tall or less, so never a tall desktop window) set `scroll-snap-type:y proximity` on the root and make every section a snap area (hero copy, hand, work grid, website value, contact, footer), with `scroll-snap-stop:always` on the hand so a fast flick from the hero cannot skip the fan. Mandatory was tried on 15 September 2026 and rejected: whenever the viewport is taller than a section (a tall desktop window, or an iPhone once the address bar collapses and the large viewport exceeds `100svh`) mandatory snapping pulls every scroll back to the section start and the page cannot be scrolled at all. Proximity only pulls when the scroll ends near a boundary. The work grid cards carry `reveal` and rise 40px with the second column trailing by 100ms. The hand screen carries one title only, "העבודות שלנו.", with no kicker, explanation or swipe hint (Liron: the cards explain themselves); cards are sized `min(72vw,320px,40svh)`. The work grid and the website-value section keep their natural length; nothing uses scroll-snap, so scrolling stays free and the iOS address-bar height change never makes a section jump. The phone hero is a hand of cards, fanned at 7.5° per card so the edges of the hand stay inside the screen: one card per website (its phone capture as the face, its brand color, its name with the accent square in the corner) plus a vermilion LA webs card at the back that links to the work. The front card stands upright, the rest fan behind it around a low pivot like a hand of playing cards. **Ring model (15 September 2026):** every card owns a fixed slot on a ring (0, +1, -1, +2, -2...), and a swipe turns the whole ring one slot in the finger's direction, so each card keeps its neighbours and only the card that falls off one edge re-enters behind the fan at the other edge, without a transition. A tap on a card behind turns the ring until that card is in front; a tap on the front card opens the project. The tapped card is read from the raw touch point (`elementFromPoint` on pointerdown), because phones nudge a tap toward the nearest large link and a touch on the thin edge of a card behind used to land on its bigger neighbour; keyboard activation (`event.detail === 0`) is left to the link. The first touch on the hand stops the idle shuffle, so the card under the finger stays put. Tapping one of the peeking cards under the hero headline scrolls to the fan and turns that card to the front (the link falls back to the work grid without JavaScript). ## Motion and accessibility

Reveal-on-scroll, a one-time hero entrance, hover-scrolled captures on the cards, a cursor dot with a "לצפייה" label over work (fine pointers only), magnetic pills and cross-document view transitions. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all of it and shows captures at their top. Focus rings are 3px accent. Header controls sit in translucent pills so they read over any project color. Skip link, RTL, isolated Latin and labelled comparison controls are in place. Playwright + axe cover serious/critical WCAG 2.1 AA issues on home and a case page.

## Assets

`scripts/capture-long.cjs` captures each live site full-page at 1440px (desktop, ≤6400px tall) and 390px (phone, 1.5× → 585px wide, ≤6000px tall), converts to WebP and records height, dominant background colors, CSS custom properties and computed font families in `docs/project-longcaptures.json`. The build reads that file for image dimensions. `*-desktop.webp` viewport captures are kept for `og:image`.


## Mobile card ordering (14 September 2026)

The all-work red card is the last item in the hand and now also starts at its deepest visual layer. Fan slots follow forward deck order (center, right, left, outward), while stacking follows each card's unique forward depth. This avoids circular-distance placement bringing the last card beside the first. Autoplay/swiping reaches all project cards before the all-work card, then cycles back to the first. Without JavaScript, the existing alternating CSS slots use each card's source index for stacking. A source-level cycle check verified initial depth, each front card in order, wraparound, and the static CSS fallback; build/check passed.
