## Direction

A bright, high-contrast canvas that lets the work carry the color. The studio's own surfaces are near-white paper and near-black ink with one vermilion accent. Every project section takes the client's real brand color (sampled from the live site, see `docs/project-longcaptures.json`), so darkness and color appear only where the work brings them. This follows what Israeli studios serving brands and small businesses do (light canvases), rather than the dark canvases common among startup-facing studios.

Identity is typographic: the live-text wordmark `LA webs` is set in Frank Ruhl Libre with a vermilion square "period" that recurs as list marker and cursor dot. (16 September 2026, craft-floor pass: the kicker lines above headings were retired everywhere, the hero, the contact block and the before/after title, because the headings carry their own weight; the section numerals in the website-value list went for the same reason, and the white pills there lost their 1px border so elevation is declared once, by the shadow.) An outlined LA monogram from the same font at weight 800 is used for the favicon, the mobile fan's back card and the homepage sharing card (`scripts/share-card.html`, rendered to `public/images/la-webs-share-v3.jpg`: three project cards on a vermilion block beside the headline on paper). The header/footer wordmarks remain live text and case pages retain their own sharing images. See `docs/monogram.md` for asset sources and regeneration. The previous asterisk mark was retired.

## Phones

Phones get their own composition rather than a squeezed desktop. **Screen rhythm (15 September 2026):** on phones the text hero and the hand of cards are two separate full screens (`min-height:min(100svh,900px)`, content centred; the cap keeps tall desktop windows from inflating), and the contact block is a full screen at every width. The phone hero headline scales with both width and height (`min(14.5vw,9svh)`) so it fills its screen. The only button on the first screen is the WhatsApp pill (the work link is a text link, hidden on phones), and the top of a five-card fan (`.hero-peek`, the same card faces as the hand, a link to the work grid) rises from the bottom edge of the first screen, so the first thing a visitor sees is real work and the invitation to scroll is the cards themselves rather than a text cue. **Peek to fan (scroll-triggered, not scroll-linked):** the hand's cards are hidden (`is-waiting`) until the hand is 30% in view; then the script measures the peek and the fan, sets `--from-y` and `--from-s`, fades the peek and replays the `deal` keyframe from the peek's place and size with a small overshoot, staggered by fan position. Leaving the screen resets it, so the deal replays on every visit. Nothing is tied to scroll position, which is what felt jerky on iPhones before. **Landing on screens:** phones (760px wide or less **and** 900px tall or less, so never a tall desktop window) set `scroll-snap-type:y proximity` on the root and make every section a snap area (hero copy, hand, work grid, website value, contact, footer), with `scroll-snap-stop:always` on the hand so a fast flick from the hero cannot skip the fan. Mandatory was tried on 15 September 2026 and rejected: whenever the viewport is taller than a section (a tall desktop window, or an iPhone once the address bar collapses and the large viewport exceeds `100svh`) mandatory snapping pulls every scroll back to the section start and the page cannot be scrolled at all. Proximity only pulls when the scroll ends near a boundary. The work grid cards carry `reveal` and rise 40px with the second column trailing by 100ms. The hand screen carries one title only, "העבודות שלנו.", with no kicker, explanation or swipe hint (Liron: the cards explain themselves); cards are sized `min(72vw,320px,40svh)`. The work grid and the website-value section keep their natural length; nothing uses scroll-snap, so scrolling stays free and the iOS address-bar height change never makes a section jump. The phone hero is a hand of cards, fanned at 7.5° per card so the edges of the hand stay inside the screen: one card per website (its phone capture as the face, its brand color, its name with the accent square in the corner) plus a vermilion LA webs card at the back that links to the work. The front card stands upright, the rest fan behind it around a low pivot like a hand of playing cards. **Ring model (15 September 2026):** every card owns a fixed slot on a ring (0, +1, -1, +2, -2...), and a swipe turns the whole ring one slot in the finger's direction, so each card keeps its neighbours and only the card that falls off one edge re-enters behind the fan at the other edge, without a transition. A tap on a card behind turns the ring until that card is in front; a tap on the front card opens the project.
