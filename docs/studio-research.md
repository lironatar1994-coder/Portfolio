# Studio portfolio research

Research date: 10 September 2026. These are curated design references, not an objective ranking of studios. All eight principal sites were opened on their official domains and visually inspected in a live browser. Text observations were cross-checked against their public pages. The visual notes describe the viewed states; they are not a full accessibility or performance audit. No reference studio's work or assets should be presented as our own.

## Israel

### 1. Studio&more — a recognizable visual world

[Official site](https://www.nmore.co/)

- **Observed:** A saturated cobalt screen, dimensional ampersand and wordmark forms, minimal corner navigation, an obvious Works link, and a separate showreel. The current visit opened a 2025 showreel overlay, which could be dismissed to reveal the homepage. A beta site is linked, but that version was not assessed.
- **Useful principle:** Build a distinctive studio identity before adding secondary decoration. A single color and recurring recognizable form can tie the whole experience together.
- **Adaptation:** Use one original graphic motif, retain an immediately accessible work link, and let visitors choose motion content.

### 2. Butcher.studio — one arresting subject, then focused work

[Official site](https://www.butcher.studio/)

- **Observed:** An almost full-screen white goat portrait, small condensed supporting text, minimal centered navigation, and an outlined contact action spanning the bottom edge. The next section stages one large project mockup with small adjacent previews. Project descriptions identify industry and work performed.
- **Useful principle:** One memorable visual can make a stronger first impression than a collection of decorative effects. Project labels explain what the studio actually delivered.
- **Adaptation:** Stage authentic production sites prominently. Keep each project's title, scope, and live-site action visible without hover. Do not copy the studio's awards or numerical claims.

### 3. Firma — typographic confidence and a clear narrative

[Official site](https://www.firmabrands.com/)

- **Observed:** A huge custom black wordmark on white, a short centered promise, and a small top-right menu control. The content connects Brand, Product, Culture, and Service with projects described using short transformation narratives. At a 390 × 844 viewport, the wordmark and two-line promise remain legible and the menu becomes a compact circle.
- **Useful principle:** White space and deliberate scale establish confidence; project descriptions should explain purpose as well as category.
- **Adaptation:** Use bold Hebrew typography with a disciplined grid. Bring the first project closer to the mobile fold than the very spacious reference introduction.

### 4. Brandim — a useful local conversion baseline

[Official site](https://www.brandim.co.il/en/)

- **Observed:** Language switching, phone information, familiar navigation, and a persistent WhatsApp action. The work gallery uses large images in two uneven columns, readable captions, and thin rules. Web and application projects are explicitly labeled alongside branding projects.
- **Useful principle:** Prospective Israeli clients benefit from immediate contact access and unambiguous service labels.
- **Adaptation:** Keep Hebrew navigation and contact paths clear, following the user's Hebrew-only requirement. Borrow the readable captions and gallery rhythm; this is a practical market baseline rather than the main aesthetic reference.

## International

### 5. BASIC/DEPT — cinematic work with quiet framing

[Official site](https://www.basicagency.com/)

- **Observed:** A full-viewport reel of client work, restrained white overlay navigation, and one circular reel action. Scrolling introduces a pale editorial section with generous spacing. The main page pairs selected project names with concise purpose-led descriptions and then expands on ongoing client engagements.
- **Useful principle:** Let the work create the visual richness while the interface stays calm.
- **Adaptation:** Use a large first production showcase and compact contextual copy. Only add a reel when authentic footage exists; a strong still composition can carry the same hierarchy.

### 6. Bürocratik — strong transitions and disciplined type

[Official site](https://www.burocratik.com/)

- **Observed:** The loaded hero presents colorful full-bleed work imagery with a bottom-anchored pill navigation. The next section switches to oversized black sans-serif text on white, followed by fine rules and editorial information. Projects carry a short descriptor, scope, and industry.
- **Useful principle:** Contrast between a rich image section and an open typographic section creates rhythm without surrounding every item with a card.
- **Adaptation:** Use a small number of deliberate scale and color changes. Preserve ordinary scrolling and readable content when motion is reduced.

### 7. Studio Freight — an organized mosaic around a calm statement

[Official site](https://studiofreight.com/)

- **Observed:** Numerous rectangular project thumbnails align to a loose grid around a centered serif sentence. Small navigation and footer labels keep the canvas open. The visible header includes Work, Info, News, and Contact. This is a different vocabulary from an oversized sans-serif manifesto.
- **Useful principle:** A portfolio can show breadth immediately while maintaining a clear central focal point.
- **Adaptation:** A carefully composed three-project cluster can suggest a studio's range without fabricating a large client list. Use intentional whitespace and consistent image treatment, rather than arbitrary overlapping mockups.

### 8. Resn — an expressive interaction with an explicit escape to work

[Official site](https://resn.co.nz/), [work page](https://resn.co.nz/work)

- **Observed:** A monochrome faceted object sits behind a quiet centered studio label. A View All Projects action appears above it, with a click-and-hold interaction cue below. At 390 × 844, the inspected interactive state filled the screen with an illustrated character and did not show conventional navigation in that captured state.
- **Useful principle:** A signature interaction can make the studio memorable, provided the route to projects remains obvious.
- **Adaptation:** Treat interaction as optional enhancement and keep mobile work/contact controls visible. The mobile observation is a reason to avoid an interaction-dependent first impression.

## Direction for this portfolio

The following are our design conclusions drawn from the references, not claims made by those studios.

1. **Make the site itself a sample of our craft.** Use a recognizable wordmark/graphic gesture, deliberate oversized typography, and a compact palette. An original art direction matters more than collecting visual tricks.
2. **Show proof early.** Put real production projects in or immediately below the hero. Use captured desktop and mobile views, meaningful titles, a short account of what was built, and a clear live-site link.
3. **Compose a sequence.** Confident introduction → large selected work → concise studio approach/services → direct contact. Alternate immersive imagery with airy text sections.
4. **Describe a studio truthfully.** Use collective service language, but add no invented team size, awards, client logos, testimonials, revenue, or conversion outcomes.
5. **Design mobile separately.** Recompose the hero, stack showcases, maintain readable captions and generous tap targets, and avoid hover-only information or horizontal-scroll traps. Each project should remain understandable as a static image.
6. **Give motion one job.** Use it to reveal and connect content. Support reduced motion, keep navigation usable immediately, and avoid mandatory intros, scroll hijacking, or decorative loading delays.
7. **Keep the content model simple.** Hard-coded typed project entries and static case-study content are sufficient. There is no need for an admin panel for this version.

## Scope and limitations

- Eight official homepages were visually inspected on desktop. Firma and one Resn interactive state were additionally inspected at 390 × 844. This is not a comprehensive mobile audit of the reference set.
- Awesome's official site returned an error during research and was omitted from the principal set. Locomotive's site returned a fetch restriction. No visual claims are made about those sites.
- Studio Freight exposes little text to the web reader; its layout observations come from the live browser. Dynamic animation can change the exact composition between visits.
- Competitor imagery is inspiration only. The build should use the user's verified production work and original graphic assets.
