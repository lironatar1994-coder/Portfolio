# Studio section: visitor questions

14 September 2026. Scope: homepage `#studio` only.

## Diagnosis

The old heading led with an implementation choice (no templates), repeated that claim in its checklist, and described the process immediately before a dedicated process section. The generic desk photograph added atmosphere but no evidence. The visitor had already seen the actual portfolio.

## References inspected

- [Focus Lab](https://www.focuslab.agency/): current homepage leads with one positioning statement and specific supporting evidence, then asks questions about what the client wants to achieve. Its large typography and open paper background provide a clear hierarchy. Adapted the visitor perspective and hierarchy, not its claims, language, client counts or positioning.
- [Firma](https://www.firmabrands.com/): organizes Brand, Product, Culture and Service around a shared business idea and describes project purposes. Adapted the discipline of a unifying idea rather than a disconnected service list. Browser capture shows its opening wordmark; content observations are from the page text.
- [BASIC/DEPT](https://www.basicagency.com/): reviewed current page text as an additional work-led agency reference.
- [Pixel](https://s-pixel.co.il/websites/): local comparison leads with commercial benefit and describes contact/conversion goals. Its numerical performance claims were not independently verified and were not used.

These are design and communication references. No conversion analytics were available; this research does not establish that a particular section increases inquiries.

## Implemented direction

Persuade: help an Israeli business owner understand the purpose of the website and begin a conversation.

Headline: שיראו את העסק. ויבינו למה לבחור בכם.

One short introduction leads to three always-visible questions: what the business offers, why choose it, and how to contact it. These are design objectives, not guarantees of commercial results. A direct link leads to the existing contact section. A compact closing line retains end-to-end service and continuing support.

The section inherits the existing typography and paper/ink/vermilion palette. It replaces the photo-and-checklist composition with an asymmetric text layout; mobile reads the headline, introduction and contact link before the question rows. No accordion, carousel or hover-only text. The generated studio photograph remains in the asset archive but is no longer used in this section. Process and contact photographs remain in use.

## User-selected revision: Focus Lab structure

The user subsequently selected the Focus Lab "What if" passage explicitly. This supersedes the three-question-and-answer concept above. The section now presents an aspirational website headline, מה אם..., five hypothetical opportunities, a short invitation and a contact anchor. It adapts the structure to website presentation, clarity, a pre-meeting introduction, easy contact and ongoing support. It does not reproduce Focus Lab's AI/M&A/industry-ranking claims. Responsive captures are `focus-desktop.png`, `focus-mobile.png` and `focus-narrow.png` in `.impeccable/review/`. Build/check and four focused desktop/mobile browser tests passed.
